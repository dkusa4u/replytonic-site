Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026  ·  Effective: November 14, 2026

Short version: ReplyTonic does not sell your data, does not store the text you process, and does not share personal information with third parties except as necessary to run the service (OpenAI for AI processing, Stripe for payments).

1. Who We Are

ReplyTonic ("we," "us," or "our") is an AI-powered communication tool available at replytonic.com and as a Chrome Extension. We help users rewrite, reply to, summarize, grammar-check, humanize, and analyze messages using AI.

Contact: privacy@replytonic.com

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used to create your account, send usage alerts, and communicate with you
  • Password — stored as a bcrypt hash; we never see your plain-text password
  • Account creation date and usage timestamps

Usage Data

  • Feature used (Rewrite, Reply, Summarize, etc.) and timestamp — to enforce daily limits and show you your usage history
  • Rewrite history (optional) — stored only if you are logged in and use the History feature; you can delete it at any time
  • Tool preference and tone selections

Text You Submit

Text you paste into ReplyTonic is sent to OpenAI's API to generate a response. We do not store the raw text you submit after the API call completes. OpenAI may retain submitted text per their own privacy policy; see openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Payment Information

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your credit card number. We receive a Stripe customer ID and subscription status to manage your plan. See stripe.com/privacy.

Chrome Extension

  • The extension reads page content only on user action (when you click the ReplyTonic button or select text)
  • No browsing history, URLs, or page content is collected or transmitted without your direct interaction
  • Extension settings and your authentication token are stored locally in chrome.storage.local on your device
  • The extension does not use cookies or tracking pixels

Automatically Collected Data

  • Standard server logs: IP address, browser type, referring URL — retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging only
  • No third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or cross-site trackers

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve the ReplyTonic service
  • Authenticate your account and enforce usage limits
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Send transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset, usage alerts)
  • Detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized access
  • Respond to your support requests

We do not use your data to train AI models, sell it to advertisers, or share it with data brokers.

4. Data Sharing

We share data only with the following service providers, and only as needed to operate the service:

  • OpenAI — receives text you submit for AI processing
  • Stripe — handles payment processing
  • Hostinger — web hosting and database infrastructure

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with advertisers.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained while your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion request
  • Usage logs — retained for 90 days, then auto-deleted
  • Rewrite history — retained until you delete it, or until account deletion
  • Payment records — retained as required by law (typically 7 years)
  • Submitted text — not retained after the API response is returned

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your rewrite history
  • Opt out of non-transactional emails via the unsubscribe link
  • Correct inaccurate data by contacting us

To exercise these rights, email privacy@replytonic.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use HTTPS for all data in transit, bcrypt for password hashing, JWT tokens for session authentication, and row-level access controls in the database. No system is 100% secure; if you discover a vulnerability, please report it to security@replytonic.com.

8. Children's Privacy

ReplyTonic is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. International Users

ReplyTonic is operated from the United States. If you access the service from outside the US, your data is transferred to and processed in the US. By using ReplyTonic, you consent to this transfer.

10. GDPR — Rights for European Users

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the following applies to you under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or applicable local law.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — to provide the ReplyTonic service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests — to maintain security, prevent fraud, and improve the service
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws (e.g., payment record retention)
  • Consent — where you have explicitly opted in (e.g., marketing emails)

Your GDPR Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your personal data where no legal obligation requires retention
  • Restriction — ask us to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing

Data Controller

ReplyTonic (replytonic.com) is the data controller for personal data collected through the service. Contact: privacy@replytonic.com

International Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States, which may not provide the same level of data protection as your home country. We rely on standard contractual clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms where required.

Supervisory Authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. For EU users, you may contact your national Data Protection Authority (DPA); for UK users, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any GDPR right, email privacy@replytonic.com. We will respond within 30 days (or 72 hours for data breach notifications where required).

11. CCPA — Rights for California Residents

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) grant you additional rights regarding your personal information.

Categories of Personal Information We Collect

  • Identifiers — email address, account credentials
  • Commercial information — subscription plan, payment history (via Stripe)
  • Internet or electronic network activity — usage logs, feature interactions, server logs
  • Geolocation data — approximate location derived from IP address (not stored)

Your California Rights

  • Right to Know — request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the business purpose, and the categories of third parties with whom we share it
  • Right to Delete — request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions
  • Right to Correct — request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing — we do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising
  • Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information — we do not use sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the service
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — exercising your CCPA rights will not result in denial of service, different prices, or different quality of service

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information
ReplyTonic does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration. We do not engage in cross-context behavioral advertising. If you have questions, contact privacy@replytonic.com.

How to Submit a California Privacy Request

California residents may submit privacy requests by emailing privacy@replytonic.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request." We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days. We may extend the response period by an additional 45 days where reasonably necessary, with notice.

Authorized Agent

You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. The authorized agent must provide written authorization signed by you, and we may still require you to verify your own identity directly with us.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, for material changes, notify registered users by email. Continued use of ReplyTonic after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
