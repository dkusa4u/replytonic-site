How It Works

4 steps. 2 seconds. Done.

No sign-up to try it. No complicated settings. Paste, pick your mode, platform, tone, and language — send.

1

Pick Your Mode

Rewrite your own message, generate a reply to someone, create a follow-up, or batch-process multiple messages at once — all in the same tool.

2

Set Platform & Tone

Select where the message is going and pick a tone. Blend two tones together for nuanced output. The AI adapts length and style for each platform automatically.

3

Get 3 Versions

ReplyTonic returns 3 polished versions side by side. Pick the one you like, copy it, or save it to your templates library for later.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What languages does it support?+
ReplyTonic supports 20+ languages — all 10 major Indian languages (Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, Urdu) plus Spanish, Arabic, French, Portuguese, Indonesian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, German, Turkish, Vietnamese, and Swahili. You can type your message in any language and get the rewritten output in any other language.
How is this different from just using ChatGPT?+
ChatGPT requires you to write a prompt explaining what you want — most people don't know how to do that well. ReplyTonic is purpose-built: 4 modes, 6 tones, 6 platforms, 20+ languages, 3 versions every time. It's faster, simpler, and built specifically for messaging. Plus, it has features ChatGPT doesn't — Reply Generator, Follow-Up AI, Bulk Rewrite, Templates Library, and a Tone Blender.
What is the Reply Generator?+
The Reply Generator is a separate mode where you paste a message you received and need to respond to. Instead of rewriting your own draft, the AI generates 3 smart reply options in your chosen tone and language — ready to send without writing a single word yourself. Perfect for follow-ups, job application responses, customer messages, or any situation where you don't know what to say back.
What is the Follow-Up Generator?+
The Follow-Up mode takes your original message and their reply, then generates the perfect next follow-up. It understands the context of the conversation — not just a generic follow-up. Great for sales outreach, job applications, client check-ins, and any situation where you need to keep a conversation moving.
What is the Tone Blender?+
The Tone Blender lets you mix two tones for a more nuanced output. For example, "Professional + Friendly" gives you a warm but polished message. "Assertive + Apologetic" helps you be firm without sounding cold. This is especially useful for messages where you need to balance competing goals — like following up assertively while still being friendly.
What is the Message Score?+
When you click Rewrite, ReplyTonic first scores your original message on three dimensions: Clarity (how clear and understandable it is), Tone (how the message might be perceived emotionally), and Professionalism (how suitable it is for the chosen platform). Low scores signal exactly why you need a rewrite — and the improved versions directly address those weak spots.
What are Templates?+
Templates let you save your best rewrites for reuse. When you get a rewrite you love — a perfect client apology, your standard invoice follow-up, your best job application tone — save it as a template. Next time you need a similar message, open Templates and load it straight into the tool. Teams users get a shared templates library for the whole team.
Is there regional pricing?+
Yes. We offer purchasing power parity pricing for users in developing economies. If you're in India, Southeast Asia, Africa, or Latin America, you'll automatically see pricing adjusted for your region — typically 40–70% lower than the standard rate. Students and non-profits get an additional 50% off.
